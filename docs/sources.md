# Sources and re-check policy

Primary sources this design uses, and how to treat facts that age. Last reviewed **November 2026**.

These links are starting points for a live check, not a frozen database of ingredient verdicts. Open the current page before you state a status, a score, or a test result.

## Regulator lists

| Source | What it is for | Re-check rule |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| [Health Canada Cosmetic Ingredient Hotlist](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/cosmetics/cosmetic-ingredient-hotlist-prohibited-restricted-ingredients.html) | Prohibited and restricted cosmetic ingredients in Canada | Open the current hotlist before stating that an ingredient is allowed, restricted, or prohibited in Canada |
| [EU CosIng](https://ec.europa.eu/growth/tools-databases/cosing/) and EU cosmetics annexes | Ingredient functions and EU prohibited or restricted lists | Open the current record. Annexes change; do not rely on a remembered status |
| [California Proposition 65](https://oehha.ca.gov/proposition-65) | Listed chemicals and warning rules in California | Check the current list and whether a warning applies to the product type. A listing is not automatically a ban |

## Certification and consumer tools

These are useful filters. They are not a substitute for a full ingredient list, and they are not themselves the law. Cite the current record for the exact SKU:

- [EWG Skin Deep](https://www.ewg.org/skindeep/) and [EWG Verified](https://www.ewg.org/ewgverified/) (consumer scoring and a verified-product program)
- [Clean Label Project](https://cleanlabelproject.org/) (independent testing programs, often used for heavy metals in foods and supplements)
- [MADE SAFE](https://www.madesafe.org/)
- [GOTS](https://global-standard.org/) and [OEKO-TEX](https://www.oeko-tex.com/) (textiles)
- [Canada Organic](https://inspection.canada.ca/en/organic-products) and [USDA Organic](https://www.ams.usda.gov/rules-regulations/organic)
- [Non-GMO Project](https://www.nongmoproject.org/)
- [EPA Safer Choice](https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice) (cleaning products)

## Independent SKU tests

Public consumer lab reports (heavy metals and similar) can inform a pick when they name the SKU, the date, and the lab. They are not regulation. Re-open the report every time you cite a figure. Do not apply one SKU's result to a different formula, size, or flavor, and do not promote it into a brand-wide verdict.

## Figures and facts that must not be treated as timeless

- The ingredient list and fragrance disclosure on a named SKU
- Lab results in parts per billion or similar units
- Certification status and consumer scores
- Entries on the Health Canada hotlist, EU annexes, and the Proposition 65 list
- Local drinking-water treatment (for example whether a city adds fluoride)
- Price, seller, and in-stock status

When you reuse one of these, confirm it against a current primary source and add today's date, or quote it as a dated figure.

## What not to use as a source

- The owner's private purchases, health records, or a child's identity
- Invented or remembered ingredient lists and lab numbers
- Login-walled pages you could not open
- Marketing copy presented as a test
- Undated social posts presented as a regulator decision
